faq_heading: Quick questions, straight answers
faqs:
  - question: How do you use AI in your content?
    answer: I use AI during research to analyze information from different sources,
      spot patterns in audience needs, identify angles I may have missed and
      find examples and case studies. I may also use it to create supporting
      assets, such as checklists and templates, to make the article more
      practical. I don't use AI to draft, to maintain a natural writing voice.
  - question: What is your writing process like?
    answer: >-
      I follow a five-part writing process:


      **Brief review and clarification:** I review the brief to understand your goals, target audience, and intended direction for the article. I also ask questions where I need more context or clarity.


      **Research:** I study first-party company information, audience discussions, user reviews, and other reliable resources. This helps me understand the audience’s pain points and knowledge level, and gather the information needed to answer their questions.


      **Outline:** I decide on the article’s angle and create a clear structure. Depending on your editorial process and how detailed the brief is, I may share the outline with you for approval before drafting.


      **Drafting:** I develop each section and optimize the article for traditional search and AI visibility where relevant.


      **Editing:** I review the draft for clarity, flow, accuracy, consistency, and alignment with your brand voice. I also fact-check claims, product details, examples, and links before submission.
  - question: How do you ensure your content is original and useful?
    answer: I research from primary sources such as first-party company information,
      subject-matter experts, credible industry publications, and user-review
      platforms. I look for questions, examples, and angles that competing
      articles have overlooked. For product-led guides, reviews, and
      comparisons, I test the software myself when access is available and use
      what I learn to provide specific, firsthand insights.
  - question: How long does it take you to write an article?
    answer: I typically need two to three business days to complete an article of
      1,000 to 1,800 words. The timeline may increase for longer articles or
      projects that require extensive research, product testing, interviews, or
      custom content assets.
  - question: What other services do you offer besides content writing?
    answer: I also update and optimize existing articles, create content briefs for
      other writers, write LinkedIn promotional copy, and repurpose articles
      into LinkedIn carousels. These are separate services and will be priced
      based on the scope of the project.
